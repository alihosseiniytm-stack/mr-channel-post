👛 Your wallet

One recovery phrase, every network below.

This wallet is fully non-custodial — MarketRadar never sees or stores your private key or 12-word recovery phrase. That means: no one (not even us) can freeze or take your funds. But it also means if you lose your recovery phrase or PIN, there is no way to recover it — not us, not support, no one. Write your recovery phrase on paper and keep it somewhere safe, never as a screenshot.
Non-custodial means we can't freeze your funds, but Tether (the company behind USDT) technically can, on any address — this isn't about MarketRadar or this wallet specifically. In practice this is rare and almost always tied to an address flagged as connected to a sanctioned entity (e.g. a sanctioned exchange), not applied broadly by country. To keep this risk low: avoid depositing directly from an exchange under sanctions, and consider keeping part of your balance in BTC or SOL instead of only USDT — those have no central issuer that can freeze an address at all.
